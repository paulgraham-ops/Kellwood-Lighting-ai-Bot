# Kellwood Lighting — Company & Product Research

Research notes for issue #2, to inform the AI bot's knowledge base.

Source: [kellwoodlighting.co.uk](https://www.kellwoodlighting.co.uk/)

## Company Overview

- **History**: Established in 2012 as a specialist division within an engineering group. Initially focused on turnkey lighting upgrades installed by sister company Kellwood Electrical. By 2017, shifted toward consultative services, specializing in bespoke solutions for unique applications (e.g. high-temperature industrial lighting, chlorine-resistant pool fixtures). In 2024, Kellwood Lighting became an independent company.
- **Headquarters**: Unit 7, Catherinefield Industrial Estate, Dumfries, Scotland. Phone: 01387 255 816.
- **Certifications**: ISO 9001, ISO 14001.
- **Notable clients**: Wabtec, Loganair, DuPont, Santander, NHS.
- **Core values**: fair pricing, honest dealings with stakeholders, customer prioritization, sustainability, investment in skilled personnel.
- **Specialisms**: bespoke, application-specific lighting solutions; lighting design, consultancy, and support; wireless lighting controls; custom design services.

## Sectors Served

- **Industrial**: factories, warehouses, cold stores, food production, foundries
- **Sports**: tennis, swimming pools, athletics, football, rugby
- **Hazardous areas**: oil refineries, chemical storage, grain processing (ATEX-certified equipment)
- **External**: street lighting, car parks, airport aprons, security
- **Commercial**: offices, retail, healthcare, education

## Product Tiers

Three tiers across most ranges: **Economy** (cheaper, still high quality), **General Purpose**, and **Premium**.
